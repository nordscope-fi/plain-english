# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise the code, the rule or the argument, never the person.

## Specifics

Expected: welcoming language, respect for differing viewpoints, graceful acceptance of criticism, focus on what is best for people using the project.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing others' private information, and anything else a reasonable professional would judge inappropriate.

This project lints English writing, so it attracts strong opinions about English writing. Disagree about a rule as much as you like. Do not make it personal, and do not use a disagreement about prose as cover for disparaging how somebody writes or speaks.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report to peter@nordscope.fi. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
